# Instructions for this course workspace

This folder is the separate ISC access-certification theory course. Read README.md and COURSE_REGISTER.md to resume its work. The general ISC course elsewhere is a separate project.

The user's latest instructions take priority. For lesson work, use TEACHING_SPECIFICATION.md and author/AUTHORING_GUIDE.md, then the relevant coverage, terminology and capability records.

## Continuation

A request to continue this course normally means the next complete learner lesson named in COURSE_REGISTER.md, with its necessary assets and author record. Do not skip ahead, create lesson stubs or claim that planned topics are authored.

Prepare the next lesson's brief and primary-source checks before writing. Keep a lesson independently understandable through locally explained terms and connected reasoning. No fictional company or continuing character, no reader-facing teaching-method commentary, no external source citations in learner lessons, no fixed content quotas.

Preserve the distinction between planned coverage, documentation verification, simulated beginner author review, actual learner validation and publication. Never claim a tenant test or observed learner result that did not occur.

When a claim is unresolved, check the capability register and investigate. Qualify a verified scope or omit an unsupported claim; do not invent a result. An uncertainty in an advanced lesson does not stop unrelated verified teaching.

Use apply_patch for file changes. Keep changes scoped to this folder. Do not modify the other course, initialize a repository or publish as a side effect of authoring. Honor later explicit publication instructions without asking for the same authorization again.

After a complete lesson, reconcile its coverage, examples, terms, questions and diagrams, update the course register and identify the next lesson. Historical per-lesson records remain historical rather than being silently rewritten as current work.

Module boundaries and unassigned planning labels can change when the content needs it. Once a learner lesson is authored, preserve its permanent ID and filename; update placement separately.
