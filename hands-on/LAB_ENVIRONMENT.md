## Readiness states

Reported available: the user has access to a training tenant.

Inspected: a person has recorded the relevant screen/configuration, actor and time.

Ready for a named lab: every prerequisite for that lab has been checked and the exact test scope and starting state are known.

Executed: the named actions and results were observed and retained.

Repeatable: the lab has been reset and performed again from its declared starting state.

These states apply to a lab and setup, not to the entire tenant at once. Missing machine-account functionality does not prevent a supported manager review. Missing target-change capability prevents a real external account-removal result from passing. It does not prevent the CSV review and simulated reconciliation phase.
