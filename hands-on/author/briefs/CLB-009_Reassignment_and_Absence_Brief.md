## Recognition boundaries

Briefly distinguish admin transfer of whole selected unsigned work from reviewer's supported subject/item reassignment, without operating both on the same case. Role/group/chain/self-review variants are not a large extra exercise. Signed work cannot be reassigned by the documented admin action; no API workaround. Manager/owner data governs future normal assignment and is not corrected by a temporary redirect. Permanent departure needs both existing-work inventory and appropriate future responsibility-data maintenance.

Worked reasoning: removing the redirect leaves already transferred reviews with manager02 and does not erase earlier decision authorship. If someone configures only future cover, old existing work can remain with absent manager01.
