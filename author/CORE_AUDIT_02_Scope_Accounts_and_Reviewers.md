## Beginner explanation and continuity checks

The lesson openings pose different practical questions.007 asks why an item is absent;008 identifies the account affected;009 establishes what the permission permits;010 establishes completeness;011 and012 choose knowledgeable reviewers and trace how work reaches them. This sequence has distinct purposes even though each lesson locally explains supporting terms.

Necessary meanings are introduced before the main reasoning depends on them: encapsulation and assignment origin in007; Account Name/Account ID/privileged account in008; display name/description/metadata/privilege classification in009; baseline/reconciliation/pagination/API in010; manager correlation/reference/mapping in011; source ownership versus access ownership in012.

The diagrams are explained as dependencies, containment or investigation guides, with actual account outcomes checked separately. They do not invent a private service sequence, universal update delay or automatic successful removal.

The worked answers preserve useful uncertainty. Archive export may have arrived after collection or been omitted; additional evidence decides. A manager identity can be unavailable even with a filled reference. A later grant can fall outside an earlier agreed baseline. The lessons do not force a definite outcome from insufficient facts.

Package replacement remains a proposed arrangement requiring authorization and final retained-right checks. None of these six lessons is changed into a guaranteed overlap-preservation sequence. OQ-07 remains open.
