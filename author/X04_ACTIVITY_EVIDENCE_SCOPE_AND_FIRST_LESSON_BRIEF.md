## First lesson brief

Working question: How do you prepare and check activity evidence for a certification?

Start with a collected reporting entitlement on a known production Entra account and a blank activity view. Establish that access held, event observed and evidence displayed answer different questions.

Explain account/identity/source/connector/event locally, then use the concrete supported setup to teach the purpose of activity read permissions, correct source environment, feature enablement, successful connection, correlation/aggregation and separately verified displayed activity. No secret values or API payloads.

Use two lanes in one editable diagram: collected accounts/permissions with correct identity association, and collected activity with its source/time meaning. They meet at the actual review's context, not a claim about private ISC services or every-account attribution.

Work through a supplied source where Activity Insights was disabled. After authorized enablement, the remaining checks and documented processing, a valid identity/source observation shows activity on two days. The readout is not two logins, entitlement-use proof or the production account's exclusive use when multiple accounts contribute.

Cover a failed activity read, late/partial coverage, wrong source/person association, collected activity absent from the actual review, and genuine zero separately. Distinguish fixing integration from rebuilding a campaign; no assumed instant refresh or destructive regeneration.

The transfer case is a source with two accounts and only a recent observation window. It must not infer production administration use or90days of inactivity from the identity/source summary.
