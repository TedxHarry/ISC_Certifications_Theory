## Beginner clarity and flow

Each lesson establishes records before relying on its logic.013 makes people versus access definitions concrete.014 follows existing holdings through a saved choice.015 names the business judgment before choosing the route.016 traces conditions against actual rows and separate accounts.017 distinguishes a match from what Inclusion or Exclusion does with it.018 follows one retained and one omitted holding through all relevant choices.

Repeated local meanings for identity/account/source, entitlement/profile/role, holding, query, refinement, filter, preview and reviewer support independent reading. They perform different reasoning jobs in each lesson. Deleting these bridges to shorten a lesson would remove needed context.

Tables and diagrams carry different purposes: compare returned people/holdings, show an alternative scope choice, show role definition versus holding, show the same-account nested condition, compare inclusion/exclusion, or trace combined restrictions. Arrows are explained as relationships or reasoning, not private service timing.

The worked answers distinguish configured intent from saved selection and actual generated evidence. They also preserve what a review does not decide: omitted access is not automatically approved, selecting absent permission does not grant it, and a saved filter edit does not prove old work changed.

No new wording change was justified solely by length, repeated supporting definitions or a desire to change the surface structure. This remains an author judgment; no uncoached participant result is asserted.
