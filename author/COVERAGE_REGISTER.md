## Coverage discipline

Before drafting, identify the units and operational task rows the lesson must satisfy. Record the supporting concepts it reintroduces and what deeper treatment is deliberately assigned elsewhere.

After saving, point to actual sections and worked reasoning. A term appearing once does not establish conceptual coverage. A diagram alone does not explain every arrow.

If lessons are split, combined or moved, update the design, task map and register together. Do not preserve a module count by compressing unrelated mechanisms, or add a new lesson simply because a label appears in this table.

Use distinct questions to avoid repetition at CRT-013/014, CRT-030/039/040 and CRT-032/037/046. Preserve the identity-selection, finality and outcome distinctions even if placements change.

The course register counts learner files, not coverage rows, reference aids, branches or author records.
