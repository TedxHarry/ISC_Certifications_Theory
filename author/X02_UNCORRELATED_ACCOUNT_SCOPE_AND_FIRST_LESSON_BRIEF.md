## First complete unit

Working learner question: **How do you carry out an uncorrelated-account access review?**

Start with a production reporting account still holding viewing and export permissions after its temporary project ended. Its source record lacks an authoritative identity association. Explain the account, permissions, source and person-link distinction locally.

The approved requirement is to review this source account's access, not force every unmatched record onto an employee. Current application/account data, eligibility as individual entitlements and no other automatic/package requirement are supplied.

Use one source and one account to trace the normal flow. Explain the supported creation area and meaningful choices, source/account selection, reviewer responsibility, comment/deadline/undecided settings, preview validation and actual start. Use source-owner review with supplied knowledge/permission, not a universal default or manager fallback.

The reviewer investigates the actual purpose and approved need. The application/project owners confirm the project ended and no continuing use needs either right. Record Revoke for the two eligible rights and a useful reason, then confirm the review.

The source uses file-based account data and cannot write the change. When tracing delivery, an actual ready task and its permitted application worker are supplied. Show the two permissions removed in the application, retained account record, accurate new file/import and verification. Task closure, application state, collected state and campaign closure remain separate facts.

Success is the specified permission outcome. The account can remain uncorrelated; a nonzero unmatched count alone does not invalidate the certification. Disabling or deleting the account requires its separate authorized requirement/action.
