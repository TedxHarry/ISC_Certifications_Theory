## Beginner explanation, independence and practical value

Each lesson establishes its own review, access and relevant actor before following the mechanism.043 adds the decision and sign-off history one action at a time.044 starts with a question before naming the report/API that answers it.045 compares expected and observed records before choosing a repair.046 establishes a specific denial before selecting the supported recovery.047 turns a vague request into capability, account, decision-unit and ownership requirements.048 compares explicit criteria with evidence at the correct stage.

Technical details serve those reasoning problems. Actor is an identity reference, not a complete edit log. A report identifier differs from a certification identifier. Manager correlation differs from account ownership. A saved profile relationship differs from the permissions still on the source account. Passing an isolated test differs from establishing readiness under changed production credentials.

The examples are neutral and self-contained. Required terms are explained locally. Changed cases require new reasoning rather than recall of definitions. Necessary supporting setup repeats across independent lessons, while their main questions remain distinct. No fixed prose, table or diagram quota was imposed, and no learner-facing source list or review commentary was added.
