## Conditions, branches and worked-answer consistency

Separate supplied contexts are used rather than assuming a person simultaneously holds every example path. Individual reports-export is outside profiles/roles/automatic requirements. Requested Reporting Access contains reports-view/export; requested Report Reviewer contains that profile plus Case Read/cases-read on a second source. Correct accounts, generated inclusion, suitable reviewers and currently held access are supplied. Other retaining assignments are excluded where removal effects are discussed.

Main Revoke branches require that no included capability remains needed. The partial-need profile/role cases instead require preserving approved access and choosing the appropriate authorized assignment arrangement. The lesson does not claim an unverified replacement package already exists.

Detection recognizes a held set. Criteria assignment continues to govern its role, and Acknowledge does not request removal. LCS profile omission and role-contained profile/entitlement behavior explain why missing component items are not proof of absent actual access.

The transfer changes the business object to Payments Approver and separates invoice-read, invoice-approve and supplier-view. Whole-role removal cannot be reported as removing only invoice approval under the supplied no-overlap condition. Its automatic-origin branch directs investigation to correct data/requirements rather than changing the meaning of Acknowledge.
