## Brief prepared before drafting

Question: How do we establish that the review population is complete? Primary COV-09 develops the independently justified expected baseline, identity/account/access distinctions, actual generated contents, missing and unexpected relationships, exclusions and why matching totals/progress/samples do not establish completeness. Supporting COV-03/04/05/06/07/10/11/12/13/14 connects source data/ownership, reviewable package/origin, campaign selection, captured time and assigned-review context without completing later full campaign design, query syntax, filter combinations or correction procedures.

Begin with the practical equal-count error, explain population and baseline locally, then work through specific person/source/account/right comparisons. The business requirement and independent personnel/application observations establish expected membership, rather than copying the same potentially flawed campaign query. Explain source/identity/entitlement/campaign/certification/item and necessary package terms where used. No fictional company/person, learner source links, method/prerequisite commentary, rigid layout or fixed length.

Main inputs: at 09:00, assess PAY.EXPORT (supplier-payment report export) on production finance accounts belonging to active Accounts Payable employees. Independently checked personnel and account evidence gives 1041/finance-041, 1042/finance-042 and 1043/finance-043. Correct associations and actual source-held entitlement are supplied; individual entitlement outside packages/automatic requirements. Generated complete inspected contents instead have 1041, 1042 and 1099/finance-099. Both totals are three, only two relationships match. ISC department values used by selection omit 1043 and include 1099 despite cutoff personnel records; inspect origin/mapping/updates before claiming a root-cause fix. Missing and unexpected entries are coverage findings, not access removal instructions.

Retain a normal source-to-collected-account-to-identity-to-reviewable-object-to-selected-generated-work explanation before variations. Population includes the intended people AND their exact source/account/access; identities, accounts, entitlements, review items and assigned certifications are different counting units. Explain query/refinement/filter at conceptual level only, preconditions of the actual campaign route, package/LCS handling, uncorrelated/machine boundary accountability, generated preview versus starting, all assigned reviews, current versus captured information and authorized evidence.

Independent transfer: 14:00 warehouse adjustment review for two people across three accounts. Person 201/warehouse-201 has requested Stock Control profile with STOCK.VIEW/ADJUST; person 202 has individual STOCK.ADJUST on warehouse-202 and warehouse-202-admin. Baseline requires all three account relationships. Inspected generated details cover the profile on 201 and only ordinary 202 account, not the admin relationship. Equal two-person counts hide missing account access; no assumption each UI item maps to exactly one account. Change evidence to actual item scope including both 202 accounts, then separately change admin grant time to after cutoff. Whole-list comparison, not raw row multiplication, explains each result.

Show missing/extra/matching rows and an editable reconciliation guide. Explain pagination generically, complete extraction, correct object/campaign/engine and stable identifiers; no API syntax, universal page size, endpoint response or unsupported report columns. Sampling tests selected details but not full-population coverage. Document unresolved relationships with responsible owner and next step; do not call an exception handled merely by labelling it. No actual participant/tenant observation or production actions.

Four new author meanings intended: review population, expected baseline, reconciliation and coverage gap.
