## Saved-file language and reasoning review

The saved learner was read in contiguous portions covering the beginning, middle, diagram, comparison and complete worked answer. This was an author review after drafting, not an independent beginner assessment. The revision replaces abstract grant/processing phrases with named permissions, records and actions; introduces campaign/review/item separately; and follows employment information to state to required access before discussing exceptions.

The saved-file review led to further edits: made profile requestability conditional before depending on it, replaced an artificial question/answer transition, explained sign-off rather than merely saying confirmation, removed assignment terminology before its local explanation, and made the partial-need outcome explicit as viewing allowed and exporting removed. Normal flow and variations remain connected rather than becoming definition lists. The diagram uses everyday access/action labels, and the comparison table is shorter without removing its evidence distinction.

There is no fixed diagram/table/paragraph quota and no claim that longer prose alone establishes better learning. The technical terms remain present because the learner needs them, with familiar access/account explanations supporting their use.
