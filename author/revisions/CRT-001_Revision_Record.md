## Example assumptions and outcomes

The main report-export account is correctly associated with the person and currently represented as holding the permission. reports-export means export of reports, not administration or any unrelated permission.

The item is individually reviewable with Approve/Revoke. It is not encapsulated in a role/access profile or required by a lifecycle state, and no other requirement retains/restores the permission. These are supplied teaching conditions, not universal certifiability rules.

Historical approval was reasonable for the earlier reporting work. In the main current case, the reviewer confirms no continuing reporting task or handover. In the independent Approve variation, current approved reporting need is confirmed.

For the successful removal example, required review steps and applicable supported removal work are complete, and the correct account is independently checked as lacking reports-export. No account deletion, removal of other rights, fixed duration, retry, specific report field or private service sequence is inferred.

The later-time variation supplies review information predating an application observation. Both may be accurate; no specific snapshot refresh/recalculation behavior is promised.

The supplier-bank-detail transfer is separate. Permission allows updating supplier bank details; an approved temporary task ended, no continuing duty is confirmed and the item is independently reviewable. The additional mentioned-but-unsubstantiated continuing duty is an uncertainty branch, not proof of continuing or ended need.
