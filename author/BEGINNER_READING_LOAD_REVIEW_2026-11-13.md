November 13, 2026. Local changes, not published.

## Request and decision

The user asked to reevaluate the entire course, remove redundancy and unnecessary material, and avoid overwhelming beginners. Pause specialist expansion for this work.

The fifty saved lessons contain approximately 130,860 whitespace-delimited words, including tables and diagrams. All fifty were presented as one uninterrupted Next lesson sequence. This is a word inventory, not measured reading time or comprehension.

Most subjects are relevant. The avoidable burden is repeated depth: introductions teach later operations; diagnostic references repeat their prerequisite mechanisms; project cases repeat the complete cycle. Useful task reference does not all belong in compulsory sequential reading.

Use a main route for the normal process, separate task reference and separate practice. The content decisions below result in thirty main lessons, seventeen task references and three practice cases. These are not future quotas. Reference placement does not mean the task is rare or unimportant, nor that someone responsible for that task should skip it.

Preserve permanent IDs, filenames and direct URLs. Cut redundant prose while retaining independent explanations and operationally distinct mechanisms.

Remove the integration tutorial, duplicate evidence matrix, timestamp tutorial and extended diagnostic tour. Detailed owners remain 003/005/019/032/037 and task reference.

Remove the duplicate evidence matrix and generic troubleshooting tour. Shorten alternative delivery and wrong-account practice without inventing universal processing triggers or task recipients.

015: retain recognition of role composition and specialist account needs. Shorten manual role-maintenance procedure and repeated automatic-role ending. Preserve assignment-versus-definition reasoning and a changed case.

027: retain exact account, capability/current need, time, package scope, representative flags, recommendation reasons and activity limitations. Move the extended flag lookup to 009's context reference and update six terminology primary locations. Remove deep licensed cloud/data product details from this introductory decision explanation; X-04 remains planned depth.

Keep short contextual recaps needed for independent reading. Removing every repeated term would create a different comprehension problem.

## Future restraint

No fixed word, heading, exercise, diagram or table quotas. A new lesson must name a distinct decision or mechanism and compare it with existing owners. Specialist depth needs a concrete certification task. Prefer improving a relevant reference to another full walkthrough of the same normal flow.

A standalone aid must solve an actual lookup need; do not turn seven planned aids into seven more prose lessons. The revised main route prepares normal-flow understanding; existing reference remains necessary for its specific jobs.
