## Status meanings

Planned: scope exists, no learner prose.
Reserved next: selected for the next authoring step, not a learner stub.
Draft: actual prose exists, still under author review.
Author-reviewed: source/coverage and complete saved beginner read-through are recorded.
Learner-tested: actual uncoached participant evidence is recorded.
Published: a specific revision and verified destination are recorded.

These states are independent facts. A source check does not imply learner validation, and a publication does not manufacture a learner test. No automatic approval gate is inferred from a pending participant test.
